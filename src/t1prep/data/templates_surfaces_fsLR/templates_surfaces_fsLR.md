# fsLR surface templates

Everything needed to put T1Prep's cortical surfaces into fsLR space, which is
what fMRIPrep resamples onto for CIFTI/grayordinate output. Used by
`t1prep.fslr`; ~4 MB total.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `?h.sphere.fsLR_deformed.gii` | T1Prep's own 32k mesh carrying **fsLR** sphere coordinates. Paired with `templates_surfaces_32k/?h.sphere.freesurfer.gii` (the same mesh in the **fsaverage** frame) it forms the fixed fsaverage-to-fsLR deformation that `fslr.write_reg_sphere` interpolates through. |
| `?h.midthickness.fsLR.gii` | fsLR 32k average midthickness — the folding target Spherical Demons registers to, standing in for the `refsulc` map MSMSulc uses. |
| `?h.sphere.fsLR.gii` | Sphere for the above, same mesh and vertex order. |

## Provenance

`?h.sphere.fsLR_deformed.gii` was generated by carrying
`templates_surfaces_32k/?h.sphere.freesurfer.gii` through sMRIPrep's
fsaverage-to-fsLR deformation with `fslr.project_unproject`:

- project to `fs_?/fsaverage.?.sphere.164k_fs_?.surf.gii`
- unproject from `fs_?/fs_?-to-fs_LR_fsaverage.?_LR.spherical_std.164k_fs_?.surf.gii`

both from `smriprep/data/atlases` (HCP `standard_mesh_atlases`, CC-BY).

Deriving it this way — rather than taking HCP's ready-made
`fs_LR-deformed_to-fsaverage.?.sphere.32k_fs_LR.surf.gii` — is deliberate.
T1Prep's 32k template *is* that HCP file vertex-for-vertex, but pairing it
directly with the fsLR sphere disagrees with the 164k deformation sMRIPrep
actually applies by ~1.1 degrees. Resampling the 164k deformation instead
reproduces `wb_command -surface-sphere-project-unproject` to 0.003 mm median
(0.26 mm max) on the 100 mm sphere, so T1Prep's `desc-reg` sphere matches
sMRIPrep's. Shipping the 164k pair outright would buy 0.003 mm for ~17 MB.

`?h.midthickness.fsLR.gii` and `?h.sphere.fsLR.gii` are TemplateFlow's
`tpl-fsLR_den-32k_hemi-?_midthickness.surf.gii` and
`tpl-fsLR_hemi-?_den-32k_sphere.surf.gii`, re-encoded as GZip-compressed
float32 GIFTI. The fsLR average anatomy is *not* interchangeable with
T1Prep's own `templates_surfaces_32k/?h.central.freesurfer.gii`: the two share
a mesh, but the latter carries fsaverage folding, and registering to it scores
worse against `refsulc` (0.869/0.885 vs 0.884/0.897) at higher areal
distortion.
